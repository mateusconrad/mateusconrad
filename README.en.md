## Featured project

**[FanPilot](https://github.com/mateusconrad/FanPilot)**: PC temperature monitoring and fan control from your phone. A web panel served by the PC itself shows CPU, motherboard and GPU temperatures with history charts and includes a fan-curve editor. The phone connects over the local network after pairing with a QR code. Built with C# / .NET; runs on Windows, with Linux support being tested.
